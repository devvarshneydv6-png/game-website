Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before you play.

Is NINDO: The Lost Scroll free to play?

Yes! The game is completely free to download and play during its Early Access phase on both PC and Android.

Can I play the game offline?

Yes! Nindo: The Lost Scroll is a fully offline single-player experience. No internet connection is required once downloaded.

What are the system requirements?

Because it's a 2D platformer, it's highly optimized! Any PC with at least 4GB of RAM, or any Android device with 2GB of RAM (Android 8.0+) will run the game smoothly.

How many levels are there currently?

Currently, there are 2 massive levels available in Early Access! We are actively working on building even more regions and epic boss fights.
